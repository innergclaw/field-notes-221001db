OFFICIAL written memo. Unlike the personal notes in this handbook, this section summarizes an official management memorandum. Follow it as written.

Source: Memorandum dated Oct 21, 2026, from AUS Management (Heather Zambrana, Account Manager, and Judea Freeman, Assistant Account Manager), to all Security Personnel, SOC Operators, and Security Supervisors. Subject: AirTag Master Key Tracking, Mandatory Roles, and Disciplinary Consequences. Signed by Nasirr Mayo 10/23/2026.

Purpose

Apple AirTags are on all site Master Keys, building keys, and specialized key rings at all locations (3025 The Bulletin Building, 3737 Market, and GTIC). Goal: total accountability, prevent loss or unauthorized possession, physical security compliance, and protect client assets. Master Keys grant elevated access to high-security areas, labs, and infrastructure rooms. Effective immediately.

Roles

RoleFocusDuties
SOC OperatorAsset Auditing & Location MonitoringMaintain SOC Key Box inventory; perform digital/physical audits every shift change; monitor active AirTag locations via management consoles.
Security SupervisorOversight & Protocol ComplianceVerify key check-out/check-in logs; random spot checks of key rings and AirTags; immediately investigate missing or unreturned keys.
Front Desk / Patrol OfficerCustody & Physical EscortNever leave Master Keys unattended; return key rings immediately post-task; verify AirTag is intact before sign-out and on return.

SOC Operators

  • Ensure all Master Keys (including GTIC Master Key #35, Floor Master Keys, and IDF/iSTAR Keys) are physically in the SOC Key Box at the start and end of every shift.
  • Verify every Master Key ring has its assigned AirTag attached and undamaged.
  • Document every key movement in the Master Key Sign-Out Log: time out, officer name, purpose of use, exact time returned.

Security Supervisors

  • Audit key logs daily so entries are accurate and match C-Cure/Envoy badge records.
  • Immediate escalation point for AirTag out-of-range alerts, missing alerts, or unauthorized departure from site boundaries.
  • No contractor, temporary staff, or unverified employee gets a Master Key without direct security escort and management pre-approval.

Security Professionals / Officers

Keep physical control of assigned Master Keys at all times during escorts or service tasks. Keys stay secured to the belt or uniform lanyard; never left on desks, counters, inside unlocked lock boxes, or handed to another person without SOC re-logging.

Mandatory AirTag protocols

  1. No tampering: never remove, open, unmount, disable, or modify an AirTag.
  2. Loss notification: if a Master Key or AirTag cannot be located within 5 minutes, the officer in possession must immediately notify the SOC Operator and Security Management.
  3. No off-site travel: Master Keys must never leave site property; taking one off-site at end of shift is a severe security breach.
  4. Propping and unattended access: doors opened with Master Keys (e.g. QC Labs, critical doors under maintenance) must be re-locked immediately after entry/exit; propping doors or bypassing locks is forbidden unless authorized in writing by Client Management.

Discipline (violations will not be tolerated)

Violations include: failing to sign out/in a key ring in the SOC log; removing, disabling, or tampering with an AirTag; leaving keys unattended or giving them to unauthorized personnel; taking keys off-site after shift; failing to report a lost or misplaced key ring immediately.

ViolationConsequence
First offense / minor failure to logFormal written counseling / re-training, Conversation for Record in file.
Loss of key / off-site removal / AirTag tamperingImmediate written warning, suspension, or termination, up to removal from the account at client request.

As supervisor on your shift

  • Start and end of shift: count the key box, including GTIC Master Key #35.
  • Check AirTags are attached and undamaged.
  • Make sure every key movement is logged.
  • Audit the log daily against C-Cure/Envoy records.
  • You are the escalation point for AirTag alerts.
  • 5 minute missing rule: not found in 5 minutes means notify SOC and Security Management immediately.
  • No keys to contractors or temps without escort and management pre-approval.
